From line `38` to line `58` at `main` function, we can see how the two machines exchange the `session key`.

First, the client send random 8 bytes contained in `array` to server and the server will also send back random 8 bytes containing in `array2`. After that, they will `xor` 2 arrays together and assign to `array3`. After got the array3's value, they converted 8 bytes of it to `64-bit unsigned integer` and assigned to `seed`, then the sessionKey was created by hashing the `array4` containing 8 random numbers. After having `seed` and `sessionKey`, they encrypted the `sessionKey` with the key which was generated by function `GenerateECBKey` has `seed` as a parameter and sent to server.
